# SmartRail — Requirement Prioritisation (MoSCoW)

> **One system-wide scope.** This is the single consolidated MoSCoW for the whole SmartRail platform.
> The individual stakeholder reports are inputs to it, not scopes of their own.

This document prioritises requirements for the whole SmartRail platform by **necessity to the product**,
not by build capacity. The four bands are defined in the section headings below: **Must** is the product
is not viable without these items; **Should** is painful to omit but the product still works without
them; **Could** is desirable with no real loss if dropped; **Won't** is explicitly not part of this
system. Selecting the subset that forms a deliverable MVP for a single engineer is a **separate, later
artifact** and is explicitly outside this document's job. A large Must band here is therefore a statement
about the product, not a delivery commitment.

**Status:** consolidated. This document merges the 16 stakeholder reports
(`passenger/*`, `train-crew/*`, `station/*`, `central-control/*`, `catering-provider/*`) against
`final-assessment-brief.md` and the files under `docs/discovery/`. It is a living document: items are
deduplicated and updated in place, never appended per stakeholder.

## How to read this document

This is one scope for one system, not sixteen scopes side by side. Where several stakeholders asked for
the same thing — a single source of truth, a "last updated" indicator, proactive alerts, an accessibility
baseline, offline behaviour, an audit log — the request has been merged into **one item**, and the
`Serves` column lists every stakeholder it serves. The four MoSCoW sections classify each item against
the **whole platform**, not against one persona. Anything I inferred rather than found in the sources is
marked **[A]**; where the sources disagreed, the ruling that settled them is carried in the wording of
the item it affects. Throughout this document, `#N` cites the product owner's numbered decision N;
those decisions were taken in conversation and are recorded turn by turn in
`docs/ai-usage/AI-INTERACTION-LOG.md`. Every item states the pain it addresses and why it is where it is. The section
**Cross-cutting / shared capabilities** lists the merged items so a reader can see at a glance which
single decisions carry the most stakeholders. Figures in this project are illustrative, carried from the discovery, and unvalidated; they are not used to size, gate or justify scope (see **Illustrative figures carried from the discovery**). The Station Master is a **sourced role rather than an assumption**: it is
documented in full in `docs/discovery/station-master.md` and listed as **Accepted** alongside the other
five roles in `docs/discovery/overview.md`.

## Cross-cutting / shared capabilities

persona-level "musts" collapse into them.

|---|---|---|---|
