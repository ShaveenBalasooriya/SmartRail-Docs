# SmartRail — Business Problem Overview

- The organization wants to modernize the passenger experience and improve visibility into railway operations through a new digital platform (SmartRail).

## Problem Framing

Today's passengers are not aware of what is happening across the railway operation, and do not have accurate, up-to-date information to help them travel on the current railway system.

---

## How to read these documents

We analyse the problem **one role at a time** — the domain and end users of the system, rather than
every internal stakeholder. There is one file per role, and for each we complete two phases:

1. **Discovery** — assumptions, 5 Whys, root cause, problem statement.
2. **Understanding the Impact** — who is affected and how.

> **Note on figures.** The assessment brief provides *no quantitative data* (it only states that
> "thousands of passengers" travel daily). Any number in any role file is an **illustrative
> assumption**, clearly marked, to be validated or replaced with real figures.
