# Rangana - The Station Master

> *"I can see my platform's out of use, but control only finds out when I pick up the phone — and by then the passengers are already here."*

**Face.** A man in his 50s in a railway uniform and peaked cap, radio in one hand, standing on the concourse where he can watch both the platform and the passenger display.

**Goal.** Be the railway's local authority at his station and keep it safe and usable day to day — the local face passengers deal with and the eyes and ears control relies on. In practice that means deciding when a platform can be used, marking it out of use or back in service, raising local display notices about station issues, keeping the station's own basic information current, reporting what is happening on the ground to control, and seeing the same live arrivals and departures the passengers see.

**Pain.** Station-level reality is held locally and passed on by voice, not captured as structured data: there is no tool at the station to mark a platform out of use or log an incident where and when it happens. So control keeps planning, and passengers keep arriving, on a picture that no longer matches the station — and he has no authoritative view to work from either, answering the same questions passengers ask but from staler information than the displays. Local knowledge is lost at shift handover, and the day's events — on the order of one to five reportable events a day, a meaningful share of them never captured at all — live in his head and in ad-hoc calls. He must also hold two different decisions apart: he owns whether a platform is usable, not which train uses it, and a platform he marks out of use is published only once the Network Controller verifies it.

---

*Illustrative name and face. The goal and pain are taken from the station-master discovery.*
