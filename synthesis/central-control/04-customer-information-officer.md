# Tharushi - The Customer Information Officer

> *"The system can tell passengers what changed. It can't tell them what to do about it — that part is mine, and the words have to be right."*

**Face.** A person at a desk with a message editor open in three languages, a live disruption feed beside it, phone to one ear and a half-written advisory on screen.

**Goal.** Be the human voice on top of the automation: write optional advisories attached to a disruption, context and advice, and network-wide notices, and get sensitive-incident wording right, in Sinhala, Tamil and English. She can send additional messages and correct a wrong one.

**Pain.** She writes passenger messaging that can contradict the actual running state, because she has no single source of truth to message from. She cannot pause or block the automatic structured status changes — delays, cancellations, platform changes and skipped stops always publish — so she is always working around the automation rather than with it. And the wording matters most exactly when it is hardest: after a fatality or a security event, when raw operational notes must never be published as they stand.

---

*Illustrative name and face. The goal and pain are taken from the central control discovery.*
