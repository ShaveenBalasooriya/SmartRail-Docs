# Wasantha - The Auditor

> *"My whole job is to prove what happened — and right now I can't even prove the record wasn't changed."*

**Face.** A person at a quiet desk in the corner of the operations centre, an oversight report open in front of him and a log viewer beside it.

**Goal.** Give real assurance: read every role's logs and reports, across the whole function, and show that the separation of duties held and the record can be trusted. He changes nothing.

**Pain.** He cannot give assurance because the logs are incomplete or editable: oversight is retrospective and unprovable, with no evidence that the roles stayed in their lanes. His reports are only as good as a trail he cannot vouch for, and the external regulators who receive exported reports inherit the same doubt.

---

*Illustrative name and face. The goal and pain are taken from the central control discovery.*
