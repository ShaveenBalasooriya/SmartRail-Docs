# Nadini - The Scheduler

> *"I own what the railway will do next month, but I usually find out what changed today from someone else."*

**Face.** A scheduler at a workstation walled with timetable grids and a calendar, phone beside the keyboard, reconciling the plan she owns against a running day she cannot see.

**Goal.** Keep the plan right: stations, platforms, routes, trains, timetables, calendars and planned works, including temporary date-limited changes — and assign crew, the driver and guard, to journeys. When today's reality has to become a change to the plan, she wants it to arrive as a tracked handoff rather than a phone call.

**Pain.** Changes reach her informally, if at all, so she may not learn that a station or platform has been taken out of use until trains are already scheduled there — and is forced into rework and firefighting. She owns the plan but cannot see today's reality: the running day belongs to the Network Controller and the two are not connected, so a restriction can fail to become a plan change and handoffs can be missed, duplicated or completed without a record. Once a journey enters the Controller's window it is no longer hers to edit.

---

*Illustrative name and face. The goal and pain are taken from the central control discovery.*
