# Chathura - The System Admin

> *"Everyone wants more access than they should have, and I'm the one holding the line — without a record I can prove nobody has touched."*

**Face.** An admin at a workstation with a users-and-roles screen open, an access request in his inbox and a configuration panel on the second monitor.

**Goal.** Manage users, roles and configuration, and keep the separation of duties clean: every role with exactly its own powers, no super-admin, and an audit log no one — including him — can edit or delete.

**Pain.** The roles are not cleanly separated, so he is pressured to grant broad access he should not. He carries configuration risk without operational authority: he can change who may act, never what the railway does. And because the logs are incomplete or editable, he cannot guarantee an audit trail or prove that separation of duties held.

---

*Illustrative name and face. The goal and pain are taken from the central control discovery.*
