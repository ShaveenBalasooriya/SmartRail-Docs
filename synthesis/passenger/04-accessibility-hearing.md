# Farah - The Passenger With Hearing Loss

> *"The announcement just went off and I have no idea what it said. Nobody around me looks like they know either."*

**Face.** A woman in her 20s in a bright jacket, eyes fixed on the display board and then on the faces of the people around her, reading the platform rather than listening to it.

**Goal.** Get the same journey information as every other passenger — *which train, when, from where, is it on time, where is it now, will I make my connection* — without having to hear it, through channels she can actually receive, at the moment she needs them.

**Pain.** Information that is **only auditory** is missed entirely: a PA announcement she cannot hear is not information she can use. When a change is carried only by the speaker — a platform change, a last call, a boarding call — she is excluded from it, and she cannot fall back on the audio to confirm something a display tells her. Disruptions, when information matters most, expose the gap hardest, because that is when the announcements come fastest and matter most.

---

*Illustrative name and face. The goal and pain are taken from the passenger discovery.*
