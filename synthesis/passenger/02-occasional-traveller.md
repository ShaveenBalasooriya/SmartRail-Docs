# Hannah - The Visiting Tourist

> *"I've planned this trip for months. I can't read this network like a local, so if the train changes, I need to be told what to do — I can't just wing it."*

**Face.** A 30-something first-time visitor to Sri Lanka from Europe, backpack beside her, a printed itinerary in one hand and a phone in the other, staring up at a departure board she cannot read and trying to match it against the plan she made at home.

**Goal.** Use the train to reach each stop on her itinerary — a heritage town, a coastal stretch, a hill country stay — and know, in a form she can follow, *which train, when, from where, is it on time, where is it now, will I make my connection.* What she needs most is not just the information but the confidence to act on it.

**Pain.** The highest anxiety of any passenger. She has no mental model of the network, cannot tell a routine delay from a real problem, and cannot weigh the alternatives. When a service is delayed, changed or cancelled she does not know her options, is far more likely to miss a train or a connection, and may abandon the rail journey altogether — the worst possible first impression of a country she is visiting for the first time. Information that reaches her late, sometimes only at the station, leaves her no time to re-plan.

---

*Illustrative name and face. The goal and pain are taken from the passenger discovery.*
