# Poorvi - The Passenger With Mobility Needs

> *"Tell me early, and tell me everywhere — moving to another platform is not a small thing for me."*

**Face.** A woman in her 60s seated beside a walking frame, phone propped on her knee, watching the platform clock as much as the board, giving herself extra time before she has to move.

**Goal.** Know what is happening in time to act on it — *which train, when, from where, is it on time, where is it now, will I make my connection* — without being forced to walk and search for information and without being caught out by a change announced too late to act on.

**Pain.** When information exists **only in one place**, acting on it costs her time and physical effort she may not have: she has to reach that place and get to the right position before the train does. A platform change or an unknown wait hits her harder than a passenger who can move quickly, and a change announced only at the station, at the last moment, leaves her no time to get where she needs to be. Disruptions, when information matters most, expose the gap hardest.

---

*Illustrative name and face. The goal and pain are taken from the passenger discovery.*
