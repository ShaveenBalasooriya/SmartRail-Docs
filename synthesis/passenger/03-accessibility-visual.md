# Ishan - The Passenger With Vision Loss

> *"If it's only on the screen, then for me it isn't there at all."*

**Face.** A man in his 40s standing a little back from the platform edge, one hand resting on a folded white cane, head tilted toward the loudspeaker, phone held close to his ear.

**Goal.** Get the same journey information as every other passenger — *which train, when, from where, is it on time, where is it now, will I make my connection* — without having to see it, through channels he can actually receive, at the moment he needs them.

**Pain.** Information that is **only visual** does not exist for him: a departure board, a platform sign, a small app screen are all invisible. A platform change shown only on a display can leave him waiting at the wrong platform, and when several channels disagree he cannot simply look to confirm which is right. Disruptions, the moment information matters most, expose the gap hardest — and a crowded, noisy platform also makes the spoken channel an unreliable substitute.

---

*Illustrative name and face. The goal and pain are taken from the passenger discovery.*
